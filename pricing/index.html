FAQ

Got questions?

Can I try the gym before joining?

Yes. Book a free trial using the form and we will confirm a time with you.

Do I need to bring anything?

Bring a towel, water and training shoes. Lockers are available on Standard and Premium.

Are the prices final?

Listed prices exclude 5% GST. A one-time registration fee applies and is waived on yearly plans.

Do you have a women-only batch?

Yes, with a female trainer on the floor. See the timings section for the schedule.

Can I freeze or transfer my membership?

Ask at the front desk. Policies depend on your plan length.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes. 12 and 24 session packages are available, or add 2 sessions a month with Premium.
